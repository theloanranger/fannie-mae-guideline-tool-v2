# Corrected MI Savings Calculation

## ⚠️ Important: MI Drops Off!

Mortgage Insurance does NOT last 30 years. It automatically terminates when:
- **LTV reaches 78%** (automatic cancellation required by law)
- **LTV reaches 80%** (borrower can request cancellation)
- Typically happens in **5-7 years** with appreciation + paydown

## 🏠 Faster MI Removal Strategies

### Strategy 1: Extra Principal Payments
Pay extra $200/month:
- Reaches 80% LTV in **4.5 years** (saves 1.5 years MI)
- Saves additional ~$5,000 in MI

### Strategy 2: Wait for Appreciation
In hot markets (5-7% annual appreciation):
- Can reach 80% LTV in **3-4 years**
- California coastal markets often appreciate faster

### Strategy 3: Reappraisal
After 2 years of ownership:
- Order new appraisal if home value increased
- If LTV ≤ 80%, MI can be removed
- Cost: ~$500 appraisal fee
- Potential savings: Thousands

### Strategy 4: Refinance
If rates drop OR value increases significantly:
- Refinance to 80% LTV or lower
- Eliminates MI immediately
- May also get lower interest rate
